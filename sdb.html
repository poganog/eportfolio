Spatial Databases

Festa da Regueifa: a Spatial Database Example

For the final assignment of Spatial Databases, I decided to create a Spatial Database for the 2026 Edition of Festa da Regueifa, the most important celebration in the Portuguese City of Valongo.

I created 5 tables, namely event_types, events, festival_days, structure_categories, structures, which should be enough to describe all the events taking place and provide information on a spatio-temporal level.
